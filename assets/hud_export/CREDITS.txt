UI ART
Frames, orbs, slots, panels, keys, stars, plates and minimap are derived from the CinderUI v2.0 GUI kit,
modified for Drugs Survival (dust/grime layer, orb gems replaced with gold weed-leaf ornaments, mana fill recoloured purple,
added danger line, highlight/disabled overlays and mix glow). The CinderUI license allows use and modification in a game,
including commercial games; the kit itself (or these files as a standalone UI pack) must not be resold or redistributed.
